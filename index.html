Our story

Built by fighters.
For everyone.

Hi, we're Liz and Sam — and we're so glad you're here. We've been part of the Twickenham community for a few years now, and we absolutely love it.

We're both private kickboxing instructors with over 30 years of combined experience, and a deep passion for helping others achieve their fitness and kickboxing goals — building strength, determination, diligence, confidence, self-respect, and respect for others along the way.

We believe in strong community, and love the idea of adding to it the best way we know how: getting people active and strong — body and mind. We work with both adults and kids, with opportunities for all ages.

Do I need any experience to join?+
Absolutely not. We welcome complete beginners at every session — in fact, most of our members started with zero martial arts experience. Sam and Liz will meet you exactly where you are and build from there.
What do I wear or bring to my first session?+
Just wear comfortable workout clothes — shorts and a t-shirt are perfect. All equipment is provided including gloves and hand wraps, so you don't need to bring or buy anything for your first session.
Is the first session really free?+
Yes — completely free, with no strings attached. No credit card, no contract, no obligation. We're confident that once you've tried a session with Sam or Liz, you'll want to come back.
What age are the junior sessions for?+
Our juniors programme caters to children from around age 6 upwards. Sessions focus on confidence, discipline, coordination, and fun alongside the martial arts. Contact us to discuss your child's specific age and needs.
What's the difference between Muay Thai and kickboxing?+
Muay Thai — the "art of eight limbs" — uses fists, elbows, knees, and shins, plus clinching techniques. Regular kickboxing typically only uses punches and kicks. Muay Thai is a more complete striking system, which is why it's the foundation of most professional MMA fighters' stand-up game.
Can I train with an injury or health condition?+
In many cases, yes — just let us know beforehand. Sam and Liz will adapt sessions around injuries or limitations wherever possible. We'd always recommend clearing things with your GP first for serious conditions, but don't assume you can't train.
Do I have to compete or spar?+
Never. The vast majority of our members train purely for fitness, confidence, and enjoyment. Sparring and competition are only offered to those who actively want them — you'll never be pushed into anything you're not comfortable with.
Where exactly are you based?+
We're at 156 Heath Road, Twickenham, TW1, serving members from across Twickenham, Teddington, Richmond, and the surrounding area. Get in touch and we'll send you full directions and parking info.
